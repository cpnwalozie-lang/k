Get a buzz on your phone

A web app can't send notifications by itself while it's closed. The way round it: put your check-ins in your phone's calendar. Your phone then buzzes at those times, and the reminder links straight back here.

Add morning check-in to Google Calendar Add evening catch-up to Google Calendar

Google Calendar opens with a daily event ready. Tap Save. Then open the event and set its notification to “At time of event” so it buzzes right on time.

Or use your phone's own reminders

iPhone: open Reminders, add “Check in with Future Me”, tap ⓘ, turn on Date and Time, set Repeat to Daily, and paste the app link into URL.

Android: open Clock, add an alarm at your check-in time, and set the label to “Check in with Future Me”.
